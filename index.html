Meet NottsHack

48 hours, one big idea. NottsHack 2027 is a hackathon for builders, run by the Computer Science Society at the University of Nottingham Malaysia. Bring an idea, pick up the tools, and ship something that works.

Frequently Asked Questions

How Big Is a Squad?

Three to five people.

Do I Have to Be on Campus for the Entire 48 Hours?

No. The build happens remotely; only pitching day on Feb 27 is at UNMC.

What Will We Build?

Solutions to industry-set challenges across two sponsor tracks.

Is Food Included?

Yes, meals are provided on site.
